About the Event

The Random High Class of 2026 is celebrating these amazing four years with a beautiful sunset by the ocean! Enjoy beach volleyball, free dinner, music, and a lantern release as we celebrate friendships and memories that will last a lifetime.

  • Where: South Miami Beach
  • When: Friday, May 23rd, 2026, from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM
  • What’s included: Free BBQ dinner, drinks, music, games, and lanterns!
