Sport Tournament

Our school is organizing an exciting **Sports Tournament** from **Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM**. The event will feature a variety of games and competitions, including football, basketball, cricket, badminton, and athletics. Each day will highlight different sports, giving every student a chance to participate and show their talent. Monday will begin with the opening ceremony and track events, followed by team games throughout the week. The tournament aims to promote teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship among students. Friday will mark the grand finale with finals and the prize distribution ceremony. Join us for a week full of energy, enthusiasm, and healthy competition!
