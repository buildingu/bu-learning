2026 Academies Science Olympiad Invitational

About the Event

Join us at the Academies of Loudoun for the first Science Olympiad tournament in Loudoun County! This invitational will be held in January 2026 for middle school teams in Virginia. All events will be held at this invitational. Food and transportation are not provided.

More information including maps, price, and expectations during the event will be released in September 2025, after the official rulebook releases.

Event Details

What Division B (Middle School) Science Olympiad Invitational
