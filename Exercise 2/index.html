Science Olympiad Invitational

About the Event

Get ready for a day of discovery and innovation at our Annual School Science Fair! Students from all grades will showcase their incredible projects, ranging from environmental solutions to cutting-edge technology. Come and be amazed by the creativity and scientific prowess of our talented students.

This event aims to foster a love for science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) among our students and the wider community.
