Troy High School Annual Science Fair

About the Event

Join us for the most exciting science event of the year! The Troy High School Annual Science Fair showcases innovative projects from our talented students. This year's theme is "Sustainable Solutions for a Better Tomorrow."

Explore fascinating exhibits, participate in interactive demonstrations, and learn from our young scientists. The event will feature special guest judges from local universities and tech companies.

Event Details

Date May 15, 2024
Time 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM
Location Troy High School Gymnasium and Science Wing
Who's Invited All students, parents, faculty, and community members
