Academic Excellence Debate Tournament

October 18-19, 2025 | West Windsor-Plainsboro High School South

Event Overview

Join us for the most prestigious high school debate tournament of the year! This two-day event brings together the brightest minds from across the region to compete in various debate formats including Parliamentary, Lincoln-Douglas, and Public Forum debates.

🏆 Grand Prize: $2,500 Scholarship + Trophy

Plus individual category winners receive $500 scholarships each!
