Welcome to Westside High School Spring Showcase 2026


Event Overview

The Westside High School Spring Showcase is an annual community event featuring student performances, local vendors, and fundraising activities. This year's proceeds will support after-school tutoring programs and educational resources for underserved students in the local area.

Who's Invited?

Students, families, staff members, alumni, and community members are welcome to attend the Spring Showcase.



Event Details

Date: Saturday, April 18, 2026

Time: 4:00pm - 8:00pm

Location: Westside High School Auditorium

Admission: $5 Students | $10 Adults | Free for children under 5


Featured Activities

  • Bollywood and contemporary dance performances
  • Student speech and debate exhibition matches
  • Silent auction featuring donations from local businesses
  • Food trucks and cultural food booths


Registration Information

Participants interested in performing or volunteering must register by April 1, 2026. Vendor applications are accepted until March 25, 2026. Refund requests must be submitted no later than April 11, 2026. Ticket fees are non-refundable after this date. Event organizers reserve the right to modify the schedule due to unforeseen circumstances.
