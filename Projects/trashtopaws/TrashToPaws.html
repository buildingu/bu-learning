Trash To Paws Drive

About Trash To Paws

Trash to Paws collects recycled materials such as clothes and transform them into dog toys for local animal shelters in the community. We have donated dozens of toys to shelters and plan on continuing to help animals while also reducing waste.

Event Information

Join us on March 18th at 4pm during Key Club! You will get to participate in the process of creating dog toys from recycled clothing that will eventually be donated to local shelters
