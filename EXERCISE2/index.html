About the Event

Join us for the most anticipated academic event of the year! Our Annual Debate Tournament brings together the brightest minds from schools across the region to engage in thought-provoking discussions on current global issues.

Date May 15-17, 2025
Venue Grand Auditorium, Evergreen High School
Time 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (daily)

  • High school students (grades 9-12)
  • Teachers and debate coaches
  • Parents and guardians
  • Community members interested in academic debates
