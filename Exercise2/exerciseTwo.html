Annual School Debate Tournament

Event Details

Join us for Meridian's annual school debate tournament! Anyone and everyone is welcome so come, try your best, and have a great time. The debate will be hosted from 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM on July 19th, 2025 (7/19/25).


The event is being hosted on Meridian World School's campus in the Mays Building Cafeteria. Here, we will have debate brackets with each contestant matched up against another in a classic bracket format. The winner of the event gets a $100 prize!
